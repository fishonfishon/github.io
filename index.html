Saltwater Shore Fishing Simulator — Prototype

Choose your set-up based on the type of saltwater fish you want like in real life. Then cast your lure by whipping your phone while holding the cast button. Casting distance is entirely dependant on how well your setup matches your whip. Once you have casted, you can either let your lure slowly sink to the bottom where you can start jigging by jigging your phone like a rod, or you can slowly reel in to simulate trolling, or you may do both! There is also a current that will affect the trajectory of your lure. Your caught fishes will be in your icebox on the bottom. Good luck!

Terrain: shallow shelf near shore, a steeper drop starting around 120ft out, holding 130–190ft deep through the 160–300ft band, then a second steep drop reaching 300ft+ by roughly 360ft out. Fish spawn relative to the ACTUAL water depth at their location, not a fixed absolute depth — a species that prefers "70–90% down" sits near 100ft where the bottom is 120ft, not off in some fixed deep zone. When fighting a hooked fish, reeling pulls it toward the surface first (it may breach with a splash near the top) rather than following the normal shore-ward swing. Haptics use the Vibration API, which only Android Chrome supports — iOS Safari has never implemented it on any version. iPhone testers instead get a screen-flash pulse in its place; a real iOS build will need native haptics (Core Haptics / UIFeedbackGenerator) to get true vibration.
