Saltwater Shore Fishing Simulator — Prototype

Choose your set-up based on the type of saltwater fish you want like in real life. Then cast your lure by whipping your phone while holding the cast button. Casting distance is entirely dependant on how well your setup matches your whip. Once you have casted, you can either let your lure slowly sink to the bottom where you can start jigging by jigging your phone like a rod, or you can slowly reel in to simulate trolling, or you may do both! There is also a current that will affect the trajectory of your lure. Your caught fishes will be in your icebox on the bottom. Good luck!

Terrain is randomized fresh every time the page loads — shallow near shore, monotonically deeper heading out, with randomized plateaus and steep drops for a staircase-like seafloor rather than a smooth ramp, reaching up to 500ft by the far edge of the 400ft-wide map. Fish spawn relative to the ACTUAL water depth at their location, not a fixed absolute depth — a species that prefers "70–90% down" sits near the bottom 30% of whatever the local depth happens to be, wherever that is on this particular map. When fighting a hooked fish, reeling pulls it toward the surface first (it may breach with a splash near the top) rather than following the normal shore-ward swing. Haptics use the Vibration API, which only Android Chrome supports — iOS Safari has never implemented it on any version. iPhone testers instead get a screen-flash pulse in its place; a real iOS build will need native haptics (Core Haptics / UIFeedbackGenerator) to get true vibration.
