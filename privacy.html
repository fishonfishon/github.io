Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026 — Fish On! Fish On! (Saltwater Shore Fishing Simulator)

This policy explains what information Fish On! Fish On! ("the game," "we") collects when you create an account or sign in, and how that information is used. It applies only to account sign-in — it does not cover anything else you might do on this site.

What we collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect only what's needed to identify you and let you sign back in later:

  • Email/password sign-in: the email address you provide and an encrypted password. We never see or store your password in plain text — it's handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, and profile picture, as provided by Google's sign-in process. We don't request or receive access to anything else in your Google account.

We do not collect payment information, physical addresses, or phone numbers. We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind.

How we use it

Your account information is used solely to:

  • Let you sign in and be recognized as the same player across visits
  • Keep your account distinct from other players'

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with any third party except the service providers described below, which are necessary to make sign-in work at all.

Who we share it with

Authentication is handled by Supabase, our backend provider, which stores your account record on our behalf. If you sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy also applies to that part of the process — see Google's Privacy Policy.

We don't use any other third-party services that receive your account data.

Local storage on your device

To keep you signed in between visits, your browser stores a session token locally (via localStorage). This token stays on your device and is used only to recognize your session — it isn't sent anywhere except back to our authentication provider to verify you're still signed in.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time using the account button in the game.
  • You can request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the sign-in features after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions or requests?
Contact us at support@fishonfishon.com.
